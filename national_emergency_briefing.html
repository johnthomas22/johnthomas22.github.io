Independent Climate & Nature Evidence Westminster Central Hall · 27 November 2025

The Unvarnished Evidence Delivered Directly to UK Decision-Makers

Modeled after the televised UK Government briefings used during the COVID-19 pandemic, the National Emergency Briefing brought together Britain's foremost climate physicists, military generals, intensive care doctors, ecological scientists, and economists at Westminster Central Hall. Presented directly to an audience of over 1,200 parliamentarians, peers, business leaders, and civil society delegates, the briefing presented the raw, peer-reviewed physical reality of planetary breakdown free of political sanitization.

📋 The Core Demands of the National Emergency Briefing

1. Televised National Briefing

A prime-time, televised national briefing on climate and nature—led by independent scientists and introduced by political leaders—to inform the British public honestly.

2. Honest Carbon Accounting

Ending the artificial omission of imported consumption emissions, international aviation, and maritime shipping from national decarbonization targets.

3. Emergency Cross-Party Mobilization

Treating climate and ecological breakdown not as a partisan election wedge, but as an existential cross-party national security priority.

4. Rapid Domestic Energy Independence

Accelerating the mass rollout of insulation, heat pumps, grid storage, and clean renewables to liberate British households from fossil fuel price volatility.
