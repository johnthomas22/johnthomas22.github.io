Physics Guide & Saturation Refutation

1. The Radiative Transfer Physics & "Saturation" Refutation

Skeptical arguments often assert that greenhouse absorption is "already 99% saturated" at the surface. In reality, infrared photons do not travel directly from the ground to space; they undergo thousands of absorption and re-emission cycles. Outgoing Longwave Radiation (OLR) only escapes to space from the Effective Radiating Altitude ($z_{\text{eff}}$ in SI metres), where optical depth drops below unity ($\tau \approx 1$). Adding CO₂ raises this escape altitude higher into colder tropospheric air. Because colder air radiates less energy by the Stefan-Boltzmann law ($\sigma T^4$), outbound flux to space drops, trapping excess heat until the surface and atmosphere warm sufficiently to restore balance.

2. Two-Layer Ocean Energy Balance Model (Held et al., Geoffroy et al.)

The simulation integrates the IPCC AR6 calibrated coupled two-box model governing the surface ocean mixed layer ($C_m$) and diffusive deep ocean ($C_d$):

C_m · (dΔT_s / dt) = ΔF - λ·ΔT_s - γ·(ΔT_s - ΔT_d)
C_d · (dΔT_d / dt) = γ·(ΔT_s - ΔT_d)

Where $F_{2\times} = 3.71\text{ W/m}^2$, $\text{ECS} = 3.00^\circ\text{C}$ per doubling ($\lambda = 1.236\text{ W/m}^2/\text{K}$), $C_m = 8.2\text{ W}\cdot\text{yr/m}^2/\text{K}$ ($\approx 75\text{ m}$ mixed layer), $C_d = 105.0\text{ W}\cdot\text{yr/m}^2/\text{K}$, and ocean uptake $\gamma = 0.73\text{ W/m}^2/\text{K}$ ($\text{TCR} \approx 1.80^\circ\text{C}$). Note that $\lambda$ already includes the Planck response, water vapor, lapse rate, and surface albedo feedbacks; no redundant water vapor forcing term is added to $\Delta F$.

3. Mathematical Addendum: Dynamic Effective Radiating Altitude

The radiating altitude is derived dynamically in SI metres using the environmental lapse rate ($\Gamma = 0.0065\text{ K/m}$):

T_eff = (OLR / σ)^(1/4)
z_eff (metres) = (T_surface - T_eff) / 0.0065

Pre-industrial (1750 CE, 278 ppm): $z_{\text{eff}} \approx 4,954\text{ m}$ at $-18.4^\circ\text{C}$. Modern day (2026 CE, 427 ppm): $z_{\text{eff}}$ rises to $\approx 5,230\text{ m}$.
