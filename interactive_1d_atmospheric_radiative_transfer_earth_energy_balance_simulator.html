Atmospheric Radiative Transfer & Saturation Physics Guide

A rigorous scientific evaluation of energy balance, OLR, and why the "CO₂ saturation" claim fails.

1. What is Outgoing Longwave Radiation (OLR) & Earth Energy Imbalance (EEI)?

Earth's climate is governed by the First Law of Thermodynamics (Conservation of Energy). The Sun radiates shortwave energy (mostly visible light). Averaged over the whole spherical surface of the globe:

Incoming Solar Flux: ~340 W/m²
Albedo (clouds, ice, air reflection ~30%): -101 W/m²
Net Absorbed Solar (ASR): ≈ 239 W/m²
Outgoing Longwave (OLR): Emitted thermal infrared
At perfect steady-state equilibrium:
OLR = ASR = 239 W/m² (EEI = 0.0 W/m²)

When greenhouse gas concentrations rise, they do not permanently hold OLR at a low value forever. Instead, they trigger a three-stage transient adjustment:

Stage A: Equilibrium. Earth radiates out exactly what it takes in: OLR = 239 W/m².
Stage B: The Carbon Spike & OLR Drop (Instantaneous Forcing). Increasing CO₂ blocks infrared from lower layers and pushes the effective escape level to a higher, colder altitude. Because cold air radiates much less thermal power ($P \propto T^4$), outbound radiation drops instantaneously (e.g. from 239 to ~235.3 W/m² for a doubling of CO₂). This creates an Earth Energy Imbalance:
EEI = Absorbed Solar (239) - OLR (235.3) = +3.7 W/m² Surplus
Earth is now taking in more energy than it emits into space.
Stage C: Thermal Relaxation to a Hotter Equilibrium. That +3.7 W/m² surplus has nowhere to go but into the planetary heat reservoir (over 90% goes into the oceans). As oceans and air warm, the surface and troposphere radiate more heat. OLR gradually recovers from 235.3 W/m² back toward 239 W/m². Once OLR reaches 239 W/m² again, the planet returns to balance—but the surface is now permanently hotter!

2. Debunking the Heartland / "CO₂ Saturation" Claim

Websites like Climate at a Glance claim that because current CO₂ absorbs ~99% of infrared at the center of its 15-micrometer band over a few hundred meters, adding more CO₂ cannot trap more heat. This claim was originally conceived by Knut Ångström in 1900 and has been refuted by modern physics for over 70 years:

A. The Atmosphere is 3-Dimensional & Stratified, Not a Single Sheet of Glass

Infrared photons emitted by Earth's surface are absorbed by CO₂ and water vapor, then re-emitted in random directions, absorbed again, and re-emitted millions of times as heat climbs upward. What governs planetary cooling is not what happens at ground level, but the Effective Emission Layer (Altitude of Escape)—the cold upper layer where air density drops low enough that photons can finally escape to space without hitting another absorber. Adding more CO₂ raises this escape altitude higher into the troposphere where the air is colder ($~-6.5^\circ\text{C/km}$ lapse rate). A colder emitter radiates less energy, trapping heat below.

B. Spectral Wings (Pressure & Doppler Broadening)

Absorption lines are not single razor-sharp spikes; they have "wings" shaped by molecular collisions and thermal motion. Even if the exact center of the 15 µm band is saturated, adding more CO₂ molecules continues to absorb strongly in the non-saturated wings (at 13–14 µm and 16–18 µm), continuously widening the wavelength window where heat is trapped.

C. Logarithmic Scaling Does NOT Mean "Negligible"

Radiative forcing follows $\Delta F = 5.35 \cdot \ln(C / C_0)$. While this is a logarithmic curve (diminishing marginal returns per individual molecule), every doubling of CO₂ still yields a massive and steady $+3.7 \text{ W/m}^2$ of heating. At planetary scale, adding $3.7 \text{ W/m}^2$ across 510 million km² represents billions of petawatts of excess thermal power.

D. The Water Vapor Amplifier

Water vapor cannot force climate changes independently on long timescales because its residence time in the atmosphere is only about 9 days (excess rain falls out). CO₂ stays for centuries. When CO₂ produces an initial warming, warmer air holds ~7% more moisture per 1°C (Clausius-Clapeyron equation). This extra water vapor acts as a powerful feedback, doubling the total equilibrium warming from CO₂ alone.
