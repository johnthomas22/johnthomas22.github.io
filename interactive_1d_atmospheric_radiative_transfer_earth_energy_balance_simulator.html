Atmospheric Radiative Transfer & Saturation Refutation Guide

Rigorous physical breakdown of why the "CO₂ is 99% saturated" argument is scientifically false.

1. Earth Energy Imbalance (EEI) & Transient OLR

Planetary temperature is governed by the global energy budget. The Sun supplies shortwave solar radiation ($340\text{ W/m}^2$). Earth reflects $\approx 30\%$ back to space ($101\text{ W/m}^2$). The remaining absorbed radiation warms the planet:

Net Absorbed Solar (ASR): ≈ 239.0 W/m²
Fixed planetary input baseline
Outgoing Longwave Radiation (OLR):
Thermal IR escaping to space
In Equilibrium: OLR = ASR = 239.0 W/m²
The Transient OLR Drop: When greenhouse gases increase, the lower atmosphere becomes more opaque to infrared. Thermal emission to space is forced to occur from higher, colder altitudes in the troposphere. Because colder air emits less radiation ($\sigma T^4$), outbound flux drops immediately ($\text{OLR} < 239\text{ W/m}^2$).
Earth Energy Imbalance (EEI): $\text{EEI} = \text{ASR} - \text{OLR} > 0$. Over $90\%$ of this surplus heat is absorbed by Earth's oceans.
Equilibrium Restoration: As the oceans warm the atmosphere, surface and tropospheric temperatures rise. OLR gradually increases back up to $239\text{ W/m}^2$. Once $\text{OLR} = \text{ASR}$, balance is restored, but the surface is permanently warmer.

2. The Four Fatal Flaws in the "CO₂ Saturation" Argument

A. The 3D Layered Atmosphere vs. The "Single Glass Sheet" Fallacy

Skeptical claims treat the atmosphere as a single, thin laboratory tube where absorption can saturate. But Earth's atmosphere is stratified. Photons are absorbed and re-emitted millions of times. What determines planetary heat loss is the effective radiating altitude—the height where air is thin enough ($e^{-z/H}$) for photons to escape without collision. Adding CO₂ pushes this escape level higher into colder air, reducing heat loss.

B. Spectral Line Wings (Pressure & Doppler Broadening)

Even though the very center of the $15\ \mu\text{m}$ absorption band is opaque at sea level, the wings (flanks) of the absorption lines (around $13\text{--}14\ \mu\text{m}$ and $16\text{--}18\ \mu\text{m}$) are not saturated. Higher concentrations broaden these wings, capturing thermal radiation that previously escaped.

C. Logarithmic Scaling Still Produces Steady Warming

The forcing formula is logarithmic ($\Delta F = 5.35 \cdot \ln(C/C_0)$). Diminishing marginal returns per molecule does not mean zero warming: every doubling of CO₂ produces $\approx 3.7\text{ W/m}^2$ of forcing, resulting in $\approx 2.5\text{--}4.0^\circ\text{C}$ of equilibrium warming.

D. Water Vapor is an Amplifying Feedback, Not an Alternative

Water vapor condenses and its concentration is governed by temperature (Clausius-Clapeyron equation: $\sim 7\%$ increase per $1^\circ\text{C}$). Adding CO₂ triggers warming that increases water vapor, roughly doubling the initial CO₂ warming effect.

3. Why Continuous Emissions Cause a Perpetual Energy Imbalance

Because human emissions ramp up continuously ($\approx +2.5\text{ ppm/yr}$), the radiative target continually moves upward faster than ocean thermal inertia can equilibrate. This prevents OLR from catching up to ASR, maintaining a sustained planetary energy imbalance ($+0.8\text{ to }+1.2\text{ W/m}^2$) observed by CERES satellites.

Reaching Net Zero ($0.0\text{ ppm/yr}$) freezes the atmospheric concentration, allowing the oceans to catch up over decades as committed warming runs its course and EEI decays to zero.
