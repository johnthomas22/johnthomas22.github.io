EARTH'S HEAT TRAP

Think of our atmosphere as a giant duvet. When we burn oil, coal, and gas, we make that duvet thicker. Here is what happens to our heat, our weather, and our bills.

☀️

1. Sunlight Comes In

Warm sunshine passes straight through the clean air and heats the ground, the seas, and our towns.

🛌

2. The Duvet Traps It

Earth tries to shed that heat back out to space. But carbon dioxide acts like extra wool in the duvet, bouncing heat back down.

🔥

3. The Mercury Rises

Because less heat escapes than comes in, the planet must heat up until it can push heat out again. That is global warming.

THE BIGGEST CLIMATE MYTH BUSTED

"WAIT A MINUTE... DOESN'T THE CLIMATE ALWAYS CHANGE ANYWAY?"

Yes—our planet had Ice Ages and warm spells long before humans built factories. But there is a huge catch: it has NEVER changed this fast in the last million years.

🐌

NATURAL ICE AGE WARMING

The Past 1,000,000 Years
Speed: ~1 mph (Glacial Pace)

Took 10,000 years to warm up by 4°C. Nature had thousands of years for forests, animals, and rivers to slowly drift and adapt.

CO₂ in air: Never topped 300 ppm in 800,000 years of ice core records.
🚀

HUMAN-MADE WARMING

Right Now (Last 100 Years)
Speed: 40+ mph (30× FASTER!)

Burning coal, oil, and gas warmed Earth by +1.2°C in just 100 years. That is over 30 times faster than natural Ice Age warming!

CO₂ in air: Shot straight to 427 ppm in just our grandparents' lifetimes.
💡 The Everyday Analogy: Walking down a flight of stairs takes 2 minutes. Jumping off the roof takes 2 seconds. Both take you down to the ground floor—but only one smashes everything to pieces. Speed is everything! Modern human towns, crops, and coastlines cannot move in the blink of an eye.

💡 How We Fix It & Slash Household Bills

🌬️ Cheap British Wind & Solar

New British wind power is now up to four times cheaper than burning imported gas. Clean power gives us our own reliable energy and stops foreign oil price shocks.

♨️ Smart Heat Pumps

A heat pump doesn't burn fuel—it moves heat in from outside. It delivers £3 to £4 worth of heat for every £1 of electricity used, replacing noisy, leaky gas boilers.

🏡 Cosy Home Insulation

Wrapping British homes with cavity wall and loft insulation stops heat escaping like tea in a thermos flask, cutting winter gas bills by up to 40%.

⚡ Electric Cars & Buses

Petrol engines waste 75% of their energy as useless heat out the exhaust pipe. Electric motors turn 90% of electricity straight into wheel motion, slashing running costs.
