Global Diplomacy · Paris Agreement Benchmark Weekly
Outrage + Optimism
Co-hosted by Christiana Figueres, Tom Rivett-Carnac, and Paul Dickinson

The premier international forum connecting high-stakes climate negotiations, corporate decarbonization, and psychology. The show explores how to process legitimate fury at planetary destruction while channeling it into stubborn, resolute optimism that builds systemic momentum.

⭐ Historical Significance of Christiana Figueres: As Executive Secretary of the United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC, 2010–2016), Figueres stepped in following the collapsed 2009 Copenhagen summit. Through six years of relentless international diplomacy, she united 195 nations to deliver the historic 2015 Paris Agreement—cementing the global commitment to hold warming well below 2.0 °C and actively pursue the 1.5 °C ceiling. Co-host Tom Rivett-Carnac served as her political strategist throughout the negotiations.

Regularly features candid interviews with global changemakers, heads of state, IPCC lead authors, youth organizers, and financial leaders, accompanied by sharp weekly news analysis.

National Security · Ecosystem Collapse Goalhanger
When Nature Becomes A Security Threat
Hosted by Caroline Lucas (Produced by Goalhanger on The Rest Is Politics Presents)

A landmark four-part investigative series fronted by former Green MP and Party Leader Caroline Lucas. Investigates how ecological breakdown and climate breakdown have crossed over from environmental concerns to acute national defense risks.

Centers on the suppressed government-commissioned report from the UK Joint Intelligence Committee (JIC) (coordinating MI5, MI6, and GCHQ) titled Global Biodiversity Loss, Ecosystem Collapse and National Security, which warned that ecological collapse threatens the survival of the state.

  • Defense Preparedness: Lt. Gen. Richard Nugee on British military resilience in an unstable climate.
  • Food Supply Fragility: Prof. Paul Behrens on supermarket logistics and acute UK crop vulnerabilities.
  • Geopolitics & Water Wars: Conflict multipliers and mass displacement in the Global South.

Also check occasional climate & COP deep-dives on The Rest Is Politics with Rory Stewart and Alastair Campbell.

BBC World Service · Global Reporting
The Climate Question
Presented by Jordan Dunbar, Graihagh Jackson & BBC Correspondents

Rigorous global journalism examining how human communities are adapting to and mitigating a changing climate. From floating farms in Bangladesh to heat-dome air-conditioning grids in Phoenix, each episode interrogates why we find it so difficult to save ourselves.

Unpacks international carbon markets, loss-and-damage climate finance, methane leaks from super-emitters, and agricultural resilience with local eyewitness reporting.

BBC Radio 4 · Earth Systems
Rare Earth
Hosted by rural journalist Tom Heap & ocean physicist Dr. Helen Czerski

Pairs frontline investigative reporting with world-class oceanography and biophysics. Examines critical ecological tipping points: Atlantic Meridional Overturning Circulation (AMOC) stability, Antarctic ice shelf grounding lines, deep-sea mining risks, and peat bog hydrology.

Features rigorous debates on rewilding vs. food security, nuclear power vs. baseload renewables, and legal rights for rivers and marine ecosystems.

BBC Radio 4 Solutions · Royal Geographical Society
39 Ways to Save the Planet
Reported by Tom Heap in collaboration with the Royal Geographical Society

A focused, evidence-based exploration of 39 real, scalable solutions already eliminating gigatonnes of carbon or drawing down atmospheric CO₂.

  • Natural Sinks: Restoring Scottish blanket peat, kelp farming, basalt rock dust (enhanced rock weathering).
  • Engineering Breakthroughs: Sodium-ion batteries, geothermal deep-tapping, electric commercial aviation, and smart grids.

Clean Energy · Grid Technology
Volts
Hosted by energy analyst & journalist David Roberts

The definitive, deeply technical podcast covering the clean energy transition. David Roberts conducts comprehensive, unfiltered interviews with engineers, grid operators, and policymakers driving the end of fossil fuels.

Covers high-voltage direct current (HVDC) transmission lines, thermal storage, heat pump refrigerants, industrial hydrogen, advanced fission, and market reform.

Investigative Journalism · Fossil Disinformation
Drilled
Reported by award-winning investigative journalist Amy Westervelt

A true-crime style investigative podcast holding the fossil fuel industry accountable. Drilled investigates the multi-billion-dollar public relations apparatus, greenwashing PR firms, and dark-money networks created by oil majors to manufacture doubt around climate science.

Essential listening for understanding why political action has stalled for four decades despite ironclad physical physics.
